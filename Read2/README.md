Visualization of Five Laboratory Reports Against Codex Reference Ranges

The csv files and figures in this project labelled “project 2” compare five external laboratory reports of fatty-acid composition in palm kernel oil (PKO) with the corresponding Codex minimum–maximum reference ranges represented in the processed dataset.

The Image in overall compares the reported fatty-acid profiles and highlights differences that may lead to further investigation. Some numerical results fall within the displayed reference intervals, while others fall outside them. The extent of agreement also varies across fatty acids and reports of this lab.
These observations was not interpreted initially as proof of laboratory error, sample adulteration, or incorrect sample identification. There are future plans which may require further examination of the original reports, analytical methods, sample provenance, reference-range applicability, and data completeness.

I used this project in not just to present in details the condition at hand to the management, but it demonstrates the use of Python, pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib for data cleaning, comparison, and scientific visualization in an industrial quality-control role.
